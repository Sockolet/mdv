# Markdown, simply.

MDV is a small place to read and edit your Markdown.

## A little formatting

**Bold**, *italic*, ~~strikethrough~~, and `inline code`.

> Less interface. More document.

- A directory tree on the left
- Your documents in tabs
- Editing right where you are

1. Click **Edit**.
2. Make a change.
3. Click **Save**, or **Discard** to keep the original.

### Tables

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
